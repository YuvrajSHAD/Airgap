Three jobs, one engine.

Small tables are where private data lives: a few hundred patients, a few hundred batches, a few thousand loans. That is exactly the size TabPFN was built for.

Clinical risk scoring

For lab clinicians and trial coordinators

What goes in
Blood panels, gene markers, vitals and history. Missing values are fine.
What comes out
Readmission or drug toxicity probability per patient, sorted high to low, with the three markers that moved each score.
What you hand over
A one-page summary of the cohort, written by a local model from the verified numbers only.

Machine and yield watch

For plant engineers and shop owners

What goes in
Temperature, vibration, pressure, purity, tool wear, shift and machine from your SCADA export.
What comes out
Expected yield with an 80% range, or failure likelihood. Batches that land outside their range are flagged.
What you act on
The variables driving each flag, so the fix starts at the right sensor.

Credit decisions

For credit unions and private lenders

What goes in
Past loans with their outcome, plus the new applications you want scored.
What comes out
Default probability, an approve, review or decline band, and the reasons behind each score.
What you decide
Move the cutoff and watch approvals and bad loans trade off on your own history.

Why it is instant.

TabPFN is a foundation model pretrained on millions of synthetic tables. It reads your rows as context and answers in one pass. There is nothing to train on your side.

No training run

Your data is context, not a training set. Results arrive as fast as the GPU can read the table.

No tuning

Built to run with the cable out.

Pull the network cable and everything on this page still works. These are the design rules that make that true.

  • Local server only. It listens on 127.0.0.1, so other machines cannot reach it.
  • Memory only. Uploaded files are held in RAM and gone when you close the window.
  • Local model weights. TabPFN loads from a file on disk and runs with the hub in offline mode.
  • Local writer. The summary comes from a small model running through Ollama on this machine.
  • Nothing external on the page. No CDN scripts, web fonts, analytics or tracking.
  • Checked numbers. A summary that cites a number not in the results is replaced by a plain template.

Demo files are synthetic: generated by Airgap, not real patients, plants or borrowers. Scores are decision support. A clinician, engineer or credit officer stays responsible for the decision. Check the TabPFN model licence before commercial use.
